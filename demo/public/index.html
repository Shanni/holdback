A seller agent sells this merchant's products to people and to buyer agents. For each product it picks how to sell from demand: offers it negotiates, an auction, a falling price, or a fixed price with a guarantee. Every offer, bid and purchase is a hold on the buyer's card: captured when the deal clears, released otherwise, so nobody is charged for a deal that doesn't happen.

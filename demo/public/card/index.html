How your card is used
  • Your card is saved with Stripe. Holdback never sees or stores the card number.
  • Holdback places a hold (an authorization) on it only when you make an offer, bid or buy.
  • Nothing is charged until a deal clears. Holds on offers that are declined, withdrawn or outbid are released.
